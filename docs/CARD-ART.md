# Card art

All 71 cards were painted on 2026-11-16 with FLUX 3 Image Klein (FLUX Kommunity License) through
mflux 0.24 on the owner's machines. Prompts are built by `scripts/art/build_prompts.py` from
`src/lib/cards.ts`: one world art tradition per house, and each character played by an actor whose
background is named, in rotation across the world's peoples. Every image was looked at before use.

Known imperfections in this set, for the next pass:
- Juliet (CA-001) was cast as East Asian and painted as white.
- Puck's Flower Juice (PT-003) and Apothecary's Elixir (PT-009) carry their name as a painted label.
- The three Capulet cards keep an Art Nouveau ornamental border.
- Medusa (UW-003) shows her petrified victims around her.
- The city "variant" images under public/images/variants were made from the earlier artwork.
